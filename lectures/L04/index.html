both curves shift right

When both curves shift, one of price and quantity moves for sure. The other depends on which shift is bigger.
1 · Demand shifts right.
2 · Supply shifts right. The quantity rises; here the price stays where it was.

both curves shift right: supply shifts more

The same start. This time supply shifts much more than demand.
1 · Demand shifts right a little.
2 · Supply shifts right a lot. The quantity rises; the price ends lower than before.

both curves shift right: demand shifts more

The same start. This time demand shifts much more than supply.
1 · Demand shifts right a lot.
2 · Supply shifts right a little. The quantity rises; the price ends higher than before.
so · The quantity rises for sure. The price can go either way: it depends on which shift is bigger.

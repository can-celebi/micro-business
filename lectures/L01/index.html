scarcity

more demand than is available
A resource is scarce if people want more of it than there is.
how to tell if it's scarce
imagine it were free: would there be enough for everyone?
air
free, enough for everyone → not scarce
gpus
if free, far from enough for everyone → scarce
Scarce ≠ expensive: things have prices because they are scarce. Because resources are scarce, every use has an alternative we give up → the rest of tonight is about how to choose.

show answer

Scarcity means choosing: a GPU used here cannot be used there.

the highest price you'd pay

explicit vs implicit cost

explicit
money leaves your pocket · a payment, a bill
implicit
no money changes hands, but you give up something valuable · your time, your effort, what your own resources could earn
economic cost
explicit + implicit
Implicit = mostly the time and effort you put in, or what your own money/equipment could have earned elsewhere.

explicit vs implicit cost

Mia quits her €70,000 job at Google to run her own LLM start-up for a year · revenue €80,000

explicit · she pays
cloud GPUs €30,000
office €12,000
implicit · she gives up
Google salary €70,000
accounting profit
80,000 − 42,000 = €38,000 · revenue − explicit
economic profit
80,000 − 112,000 = −€32,000 · revenue − explicit − implicit
The accountant sees a profit; the economist sees that Mia would be €32,000 better off staying at Google. Explicit 42,000; implicit 70,000; economic cost 112,000.

sunk cost

already paid, can't get back → ignore it
Money already spent that you can't get back: it's gone either way, so ignore it when you decide.
concert
a ticket you can't refund or resell
swimming pools
a 6-month membership for Vienna's pools · non-refundable
Opportunity costs are about the decisions you face now, before and after paying. The sunk payment itself cannot be changed by any decision, so it does not enter. Going to the concert still costs your evening; if you could resell the ticket, going would also cost the resale price (PS1 Q1). Next slide: an iPhone you can resell is only partly sunk.

marginal vs average

one more · per unit
marginal = what one more unit adds to the total
average = the total divided by the number of units
spotify family
€18 a month for up to 6 people · 5 people → average €3.60 each · a 6th person costs €0 extra
you
one more study hour
Decisions are about the next unit, so they need the marginal value, not the average.

positive vs normative

what is · what should be
A positive statement says what is and can be checked with facts; a normative statement says what should be.
positive
can be checked with data
normative
a value judgement: "should", "too high", "fair"
Economists try to keep the two apart: first what is, then what should be.

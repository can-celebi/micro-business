scarcity

more wanted than there is
A resource is scarce when people want more of it than there is at a price of zero, so using it one way means not using it another way.

electricity
data centres
water
cooling

The AI boom runs on scarce inputs. If they were free, everyone would want more than exists.

you

Decisions are about the next unit, so they need the marginal value, not the average.

positive vs normative

what is · what should be
A positive statement says what is and can be checked with facts; a normative statement says what should be.
positive
can be checked with data
normative
a value judgement: "should", "too high", "fair"
Economists try to keep the two apart: first what is, then what should be.

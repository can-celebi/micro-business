summary

demand / inverse demand
price → quantity wanted · quantity → price buyers would pay
supply / inverse supply
price → quantity offered · quantity → price sellers need
the price splits the gain
the total gain is the same at any price; the price decides who gets it
equilibrium
the same Q and P for demand and supply · the marginal buyer and seller pin down the price
shortage / surplus
price below / above P* · the short side is sold
